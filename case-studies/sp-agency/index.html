Case study · S P Agency, Inc. · Cincinnati, Ohio

How S P Agency went from sometimes named to named every time on ChatGPT

S P Agency is an independent insurance agency in Cincinnati that works with high-net-worth families. We asked ChatGPT the questions their future clients ask, logged out, and wrote down whether it named the agency. Here is what it said on our first check and what it says now.

“Find me a high net worth insurance agent in Ohio”

What we did

Every fact we added came from the agencyEverything here is factual and comes from the agency’s own answers to our questions. Nothing was made up for AI.rsquo;s own answers to our intake questions.

  1. Oct 18Started work. Ran a baseline of about 100 customer-style questions across AI tools.
  2. Nov 2–4Added schema markup, an llms.txt file and a Contact page FAQ written from the agency’s facts. Checked them live.
  3. Nov 5–6Matched the agency’s Google, Apple Maps, Bing and Yelp listings to the same name, city and services.
  4. Nov 6First logged-out ChatGPT check, the “from” above.
  5. Nov 13–18Claimed the agency’s ChatGPT business profile, which ChatGPT then verified.
  6. Nov 17–19Linked the agency’s official profiles in the site’s schema.
  7. Nov 23Second logged-out check, the “to” above.

The fine print

  • The site changes went live a few days before our first ChatGPT check. The listings and the ChatGPT profile came after it.
  • We can’t tell which change made the difference. AI answers vary from chat to chat, which is why we ask each question more than once.
  • ChatGPT uses rough location even when logged out. One Ohio answer said “Since you’re near the Cincinnati area.”
  • This page is about what ChatGPT says. It makes no claim about website visits or leads.
  • We keep checking every week.
