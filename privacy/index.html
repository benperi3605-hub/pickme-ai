Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information PickMe AI collects through pickme-ai.com and through our work, how we use it, and the choices you have. PickMe AI is operated by BP Answer Engine Optimization Company LLC. Questions go to ben@pickme-ai.com.

What we collect on this website

  • Usage data. We use Google Analytics and the Meta pixel to see which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are, what device they use, and whether they click to book a call or send an email.
  • What you send us. If you email us or book a call through Calendly, we receive your name, email and anything you choose to write.

This website has no sign-up form and does not ask for payment details.

How we use it

We use it to answer you, to do the work you hire us for, and to understand which pages are useful. We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run the tools above (Google, Meta and Calendly), under their own privacy policies, and anyone the law requires us to share it with.

Reddit data

We help small businesses answer customer questions on Reddit. If Reddit gives us access to its Data API, we will use it only as follows:

  • Read-only access to public posts in a small number of subreddits that match a short keyword list, about 200 requests a day.
  • From each matching post we keep only what is needed to point a business owner to it: the post's link, title, subreddit and time.
  • Business owners read the post and reply by hand from their own accounts, saying who they are. We never post, vote, or send messages through the API.
  • We do not train AI models on Reddit data, and we do not sell, license or share it.
  • If a post is deleted on Reddit, we delete our copy within 48 hours.

How long we keep it

Email and booking details are kept for as long as we work together and then deleted on request. Analytics data follows the retention settings of Google Analytics and Meta.

Your choices

You can block analytics with your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker. To see, correct or delete information we hold about you, email ben@pickme-ai.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
