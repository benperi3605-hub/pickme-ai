What is Answer Engine Optimization?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) makes your business easy for AI assistants to find, understand, and recommend. When a customer asks ChatGPT for the best bakery nearby, AEO is the reason your name is in the reply.

Is this different from SEO?

Yes, and the two work together. SEO ranks you in a list of links on Google. AEO gets you named in the AI's answer, which usually includes only two or three businesses. Most of our AEO work improves your Google ranking as well.
