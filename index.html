FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

What is Answer Engine Optimization?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the work of making your business easy for AI assistants to find, understand, and recommend. When someone asks ChatGPT for the best bakery nearby, AEO is what puts your name in the reply.

Is this different from SEO?

Yes, and the two work together. SEO helps you rank in a list of links. AEO helps you get named in the AI's answer, which usually includes only two or three businesses. Most of what we do also helps your Google ranking.

Do I need a new website?

Not always. If your current site is in good shape, we upgrade it in place. If it's slow, outdated, or hard to edit, we build you a new one. The free audit tells us which.

Can you guarantee I'll show up on ChatGPT?

No one can guarantee what an AI says. We build and maintain your site to give you the strongest possible chance, and we show you the results every month.

What does it cost?

A flat monthly fee, month to month, that you can cancel any time. The amount depends on whether your current site needs a tune-up or a rebuild, so we quote it on the free call after we've looked at your site. The audit and the call cost nothing, and you'll know the number before you decide.

How fast will I see results?

Site changes go live in the first few weeks. Each AI platform refreshes what it knows on its own schedule, so some pick up changes faster than others. Your monthly report shows exactly where you stand.
