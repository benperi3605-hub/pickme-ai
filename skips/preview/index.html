About Skip'sNew

Skip's Barbershop is your local Barbershop with a comfortable and welcoming feel and high-end execution and attention to detail

Skip's Barbershop started in South Boston, Massachusetts in 1906. It was run by Skip ScarrowSpelling waiting, then taken over by his son and then his grandson Boby. When Boby retired, Anthony Costa took the shop over and continued the long tradition of excellence.
