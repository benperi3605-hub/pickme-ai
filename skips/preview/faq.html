Questions and answers

Answered by Anthony Costa, owner of Skip's Barbershop.

Booking and walk-ins

Do you take walk-ins?
Yes, we take walk-ins.
How far ahead should I book? Can I get in the same day?
Same-day appointments are possible.
What is your cancellation policy?
We have a four-hour cancellation policy.

Cuts and shaves

Do you do fades, tapers and lineups?
Yes. We do fades and tapers (skin, low, mid and high) and lineups.
Do you cut curly or textured hair?
Yes, we cut curly and textured hair.
Do you do longer hair and all-scissor cuts?
Yes, we do longer hair and all-scissor cuts.
Do you cut kids' hair?
Yes, we cut kids' hair.
Which shaves do you offer?
We offer hot towel shaves, head shaves and face shaves.
Can I book a beard trim on its own?
Yes, you can book a beard trim on its own.

Getting to the shops

What is the nearest T stop?
Andrew station is the closest to the South Boston shop. Back Bay station is the nearest to the South End shop. There is no train station near the Providence shop.
Is there parking near the shops?
It's street parking at all shops.
Are the shops wheelchair accessible?
The Providence shop is wheelchair accessible. The South Boston shop has one step up, but you can get a wheelchair in. The South End shop is in a garden-level brownstone.

Paying and extras

How can I pay?
You can pay with cash, card, Apple Pay or Venmo.
Do you offer gift cards?
Yes, we offer gift cards online.Link waiting
Do you do weddings and group events?
Yes. We love doing group and wedding events. We call them Whiskey and Whiskers. See Whiskey and Whiskers
What languages do your barbers speak?
Mostly English, and some Spanish.
What products do you use and sell?
On the shelf, Church California is our number one product. We also carry some other products.
